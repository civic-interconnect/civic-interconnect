# Provenance

Provenance documents the origin, attribution, and processing history of data.

Civic Interconnect uses provenance records to describe source datasets,
responsible agents, and activities involved in obtaining or transforming data.

## Example: Chicago Contracts Dataset

The example uses PROV-YAML to describe:

- **Entity:** City of Chicago Contracts Dataset.
- **Agent:** City of Chicago, Department of Procurement Services.
- **Activity:** Retrieval of the dataset on December 11, 2025.

[View the Chicago Contracts PROV-YAML file](https://civic-interconnect.github.io/civic-interconnect/en/provenance/chicago_contracts.prov.yaml)

The example records provenance information.
It does not independently verify the source assertions or
establish the identity of entities mentioned in the dataset.
